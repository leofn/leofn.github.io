Open datasets, dashboards, and data experiments from my research and the LABHD-UFBA lab.

## Lattes CV Collections

- **[PGEDU/UFBA](https://leofn.github.io/lattes_pgedu/)** — Faculty Lattes CVs from the Graduate Program in Education at UFBA. Structured JSON + interactive dashboard with publication metrics, research rankings, and Google Scholar citations.
- **[PPGCS-UFBA](https://leofn.github.io/lattes_ppgcs/)** — Faculty Lattes CVs from the Graduate Program in Social Sciences at UFBA. Same pipeline — 23 researchers, consolidated dataset, interactive dashboard.

## Data Analysis

- **[Digital Brain CADE](https://leofn.github.io/Digital_Brain_CADE/)** — Computational analysis of Brazilian antitrust (CADE) data using digital methods and network analysis.

## Digital Collections

- **[Terreiros da Bahia](https://labhdufba.github.io/terreiro-map/)** — Interactive map of Afro-Brazilian religious spaces (terreiros de candomblé) in Bahia, built with OpenStreetMap/Overpass data and MapLibre GL JS.
